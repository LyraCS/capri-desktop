# Her faces, the ones that ship

Drop pictures here and they travel inside the app: the mini-box wears them, in turn, over the
expression names she has (sleepy, playing, alert…). Any `.png` / `.jpg` / `.webp` / `.gif` works; a
square crop of her face reads best, and 16:9 fills the tile exactly.

Order of use:

1. `~/Library/Application Support/capri-desktop/faces/` — the person running her, for themselves
   (tray › Mini-box › Open her faces folder). Never packaged.
2. **This folder** — what a release carries.
3. Empty — she wears the eight `assets/capri/face-*.png` her chat panel already uses.

Only put art here that you have the right to redistribute. A character, a frame from a show, or
someone's fan art is not free to ship, even when it is easy to find.
